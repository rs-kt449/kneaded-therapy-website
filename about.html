Our Story

From the therapist's chair to the mixing bowl.

Kneaded Therapy started with a psychologist who got very, very good at understanding what actually makes people feel better — and realized the answer, more often than not, involved a warm cookie.

So the couch became a metaphor, the dough became the medium, and Kneaded Therapy was born: a fun, artisanal bakery brand for a new generation. We launched with a handmade cookie-and-brownie menu, and we're growing into limited-edition "Storybook Drops" inspired by beloved fictional treats.

The name plays on "knead" (dough) and "need" (therapy) — comfort food with a wink at self-care. We take the baking seriously. We do not take ourselves too seriously.
